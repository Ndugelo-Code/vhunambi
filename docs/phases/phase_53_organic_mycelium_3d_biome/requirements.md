# 📋 PHASE 53 REQUIREMENTS: THE 3D ORGANIC MYCELIUM BIOME
## Formal EARS / RFC-2119 Requirements Specification & Verification Traceability Matrix

---

### 1. Document Scope & Specification Standards

This specification establishes the strict functional, mathematical, aesthetic, and performance requirements for **Phase 53: The 3D Organic Mycelium Biome** ("A Song as a Living Ecosystem"). 

All requirements adhere to the **Easy Approach to Requirements Syntax (EARS)**:
* **Ubiquitous**: `THE SYSTEM SHALL <system response>`
* **Event-Driven**: `WHEN <trigger>, THE SYSTEM SHALL <system response>`
* **State-Driven**: `WHILE <state>, THE SYSTEM SHALL <system response>`
* **Unwanted Behavior**: `IF <condition>, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL <system response>`
* **Optional**: `WHERE <feature is enabled>, THE SYSTEM SHALL <system response>`

Requirements conform strictly to **RFC-2119 terminology** (`SHALL`, `MUST`, `SHOULD`, `MAY`).

### 3. Functional Requirements: Subterranean Mycelium & Cross-Cluster Intersections

#### REQ-P53-MYCEL-01: Interconnected Cross-Cluster Hyphal Conduits
* **Statement**: The system SHALL generate a subterranean network of branching hyphae filaments that physically connect mutually dependent clusters, establishing that the 6 musical domains are not isolated components but an integrated living ecosystem.
* **Primary Hyphae Paths**: Thick, high-luminosity hyphal conduits SHALL connect:
  1. `melodyCore` $\leftrightarrow$ `vocalNetwork` (Vocal topline melody)
  2. `melodyCore` $\leftrightarrow$ `harmonyNetwork` (Melodic-harmonic alignment)
  3. `melodyCore` $\leftrightarrow$ `rhythmNetwork` (Rhythmic phrasing of hooks)
  4. `melodyCore` $\leftrightarrow$ `emotionNetwork` (Melodic emotional valence)
  5. `rhythmNetwork` $\leftrightarrow$ `productionNetwork` (Drum compression and stem lock)
  6. `rhythmNetwork` $\leftrightarrow$ `harmonyNetwork` (Log-drum pitch tuning)
  7. `harmonyNetwork` $\leftrightarrow$ `productionNetwork` (Reverb acoustics on chords)
  8. `vocalNetwork` $\leftrightarrow$ `emotionNetwork` (Vocal expressive urgency)

#### REQ-P53-MYCEL-02: Space-Colonization Procedural Hyphae Branching
* **Statement**: Subterranean hyphae branching SHALL be generated procedurally using a 3D Space-Colonization algorithm driven by a deterministic 64-bit song fingerprint seed (`sha256(genre + bpm + key + prompt)`).
* **Determinism**: The exact same song parameters SHALL generate identical hyphal branching paths across all app launches, while two different songs SHALL generate visually distinct ecosystems.

#### REQ-P53-MYCEL-03: Traveling Synaptic Nutrient Photons
* **Statement**: The system SHALL render traveling bioluminescent photon packets along active hyphal conduits using additive blending (`BlendMode.plus`).
* **Velocity Modulation**: Photon transit speed along each conduit SHALL scale linearly with track tempo (BPM) and current playhead section energy ($v = v_{\text{base}} \cdot (\text{BPM} / 120.0) \cdot E_{\text{section}}$).

#### REQ-P53-MYCEL-04: Network Junctions & Sprout / Pinhead Mushrooms
* **Statement**: WHERE 3 or more hyphal filaments converge, the system SHALL render a glowing nodal junction bead.
* **Sprout Mushrooms**: At the top 20% most active junction points, the system SHALL spawn miniature sprout/pinhead mushrooms ($H = 8\text{dp}-16\text{dp}$) pushing through the ground plane, adding biological depth without creating UI text pill clutter.

#### REQ-P53-MYCEL-05: Crystalline Stem-Lock Sheathing

### 4. Functional Requirements: 3D Camera, Gestures & Spatial Hierarchy

#### REQ-P53-CAM-01: Perspective Terrain Camera Angle
* **Statement**: The system SHALL render the 3D Biome using a perspective projection camera anchored in spherical coordinates $(r, \theta, \phi)$ looking toward target focal center $(x_c, y_c, z_c)$.
* **Pitch Constraint**: The camera pitch angle $\phi$ SHALL be strictly clamped between $15^\circ$ and $75^\circ$ above the ground plane, preventing disorientation, camera flipping, or clipping underneath the subterranean terrain grid.

#### REQ-P53-CAM-02: Rock-Solid 1-Finger Orbit & 2-Finger Pinch Zoom
* **Statement**: The viewport SHALL support simultaneous 1-finger drag orbit and 2-finger pinch-to-zoom without freezing or jumping:
  * 1-finger horizontal drag SHALL orbit azimuth $\theta$ continuously through $360^\circ$ at sensitivity $\Delta \theta = \Delta x \cdot 0.007\text{ rad/px}$.
  * 1-finger vertical drag SHALL adjust elevation pitch $\phi$ between $15^\circ$ and $75^\circ$ at sensitivity $\Delta \phi = \Delta y \cdot 0.007\text{ rad/px}$.
  * 2-finger pinch gesture SHALL scale camera distance $r$ smoothly between $180\text{dp}$ (macro focus) and $650\text{dp}$ (wide landscape).

#### REQ-P53-CAM-03: Zero Overlap Billboard Badges in Overview Mode
* **Statement**: In Overview Mode, the system SHALL render circular icon badges ONLY directly above the 6 Parent Mushroom Heads (zero child leaf pills displayed on the global landscape).
* **Hit-Test Safety**: Text pill collisions and overlapping rectangular cards SHALL be strictly 0% in Overview Mode.

#### REQ-P53-CAM-04: Canopy Dive Glide Animation
* **Statement**: WHEN a user taps any of the 6 Parent Mushroom Heads or its floating badge, the system SHALL smoothly glide the camera along a Catmull-Rom spline over 500ms into that mushroom's canopy ($r = 210\text{dp}$, focal center shifted to the mushroom's 3D locus).
* **Child Node Unfurling**: Upon arrival in the canopy, the mushroom's attached child leaf nodes SHALL fan out radially around the cap perimeter at arm's reach with zero mutual overlap.

#### REQ-P53-CAM-05: Floating Return to Surface Overview Button
* **Statement**: WHILE the camera is inside a mushroom canopy, the system SHALL display a floating `[ Surface Overview ]` compass pill (`Key('biome_return_overview_btn')`) in the top-left corner.
* **Return Trigger**: WHEN the user taps `Key('biome_return_overview_btn')`, the system SHALL smoothly glide the camera back to the default global perspective $(r = 380\text{dp}, \theta = 0^\circ, \phi = 35^\circ)$ and stow the child leaf nodes.

#### REQ-P53-CAM-06: Decoupled Gesture Layering
* **Statement**: The background orbit gesture detector SHALL reside on a dedicated `Positioned.fill` layer placed behind interactive HUD buttons, drawer surfaces, and floating mushroom badges. Tapping any HUD button SHALL execute instantly without triggering viewport drag or orbit deltas.

### 9. Functional Requirements: MasterMediaObject & Synthesis Pipeline Integration

#### REQ-P53-INT-01: Song Spec Compilation with 3D Mycelium Topology Manifest
* **Statement**: WHEN the user triggers synthesis from the Biome (`Key('biome_synthesize_btn')`), the system SHALL invoke `compileSongSpec` in `prompt_compiler.ts` including a `myceliumTopology` object containing cluster energy weights, active hyphal connections, and pre-generation stem-lock mappings.

#### REQ-P53-INT-02: GPU Ingestion of Pure Stems via Handler.py
* **Statement**: The synthesis payload SHALL serialize `pregenStemLocks` mappings for each cluster. If `rhythmNetwork` is set to `lockedPure44k`, the remote Runpod worker `handler.py` SHALL bypass AI audio diffusion for drums and log-drums, splicing the untouched 44.1 kHz Sketchpad audio directly prior to Stage 4 mastering.

#### REQ-P53-INT-03: Real-Time Waveform Extraction from Active Playback
* **Statement**: WHILE track playback is active in the Mini Transport Pill, the system SHALL sample instantaneous audio amplitude every $16\text{ms}$ (60 FPS) to drive the dynamic height of the waveform canvas in `BiomeClusterInspectorPanel`.

#### REQ-P53-INT-04: Dynamic Stem Isolation via StemMixerProvider
* **Statement**: WHEN a user isolates or solos a cluster in the Biome, the system SHALL command `stemMixerProvider` to mute all non-selected stems and boost the selected stem by $+2.0\text{dBFS}$, providing synchronized acoustic focus.

#### REQ-P53-INT-05: MMO State Hydration and Persistence
* **Statement**: The system SHALL serialize the active `MusicalDnaGraph` snapshot into the local Hive store (`AppConstants.mmoBoxKey`) associated with the active song's `MasterMediaObject`, ensuring instantaneous visual restoration upon re-opening the session.

### 11. Error Handling & Degradation Resilience Requirements

#### REQ-P53-ERR-01: Headless / Low-Power Device Fallback
* **Statement**: IF the target device GPU reports a frame render time exceeding $28\text{ms}$ (dropping below 35 FPS), THEN the system SHALL automatically downscale hyphal spline subdivision from 16 segments to 8 segments, maintaining fluid 60 FPS interaction.

#### REQ-P53-ERR-02: Missing Audio Stream Graceful Degradation
* **Statement**: IF audio amplitude streams are null or uninitialized (e.g., when viewing a template before synthesis), THEN the system SHALL synthesize synthetic idle respiratory waveforms driven by a smooth $0.8\text{Hz}$ sine oscillator, ensuring the Biome never appears dead or static.

#### REQ-P53-ERR-03: Rapid Multitouch Gesture Disambiguation
* **Statement**: IF the user executes simultaneous conflicting touch gestures (e.g. initiating a 2-finger zoom while tapping a mushroom cap), THEN the system SHALL prioritize camera transformation over selection, suppressing accidental drawer opens.

#### REQ-P53-ERR-04: Offline Graph Persistence Recovery
* **Statement**: IF local storage fails during session save, THEN the system SHALL retain the complete `MusicalDnaGraph` in memory and retry persistence asynchronously, ensuring zero loss of user parameter adjustments.
